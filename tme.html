GRADEY
Gradey is an intelligent, easy-to-use gradebook designed for teachers. Track student scores, compute averages, auto-rank students, and visualize performance with interactive charts. Teachers can edit names and grades inline, export reports to CSV or PDF, and manage grades per term or per subject. Its clean, school-themed interface makes grading fast, accurate, and stress-free.
PUNCTUAL
Punctual is a per-term and per-week attendance tracker that helps teachers quickly mark, track, and analyze student attendance. Its color-coded system makes it easy to see patterns at a glance, and teachers can bulk add students and weeks. Attendance data is saved automatically and can be exported for reporting purposes.
CURRICULAR
Curricular helps teachers plan and organize lessons per term and week. Teachers can create weeks with topics, objectives, materials, assessments, and homework in bulk. The intuitive interface allows for easy tracking of lesson progress, making lesson preparation efficient and structured.
CLASS SCRIBBLE
ClassScribble (originally name Notery then WYS+)is a school-focused, PowerPoint-style presentation tool for teachers and students. Create, edit, and reorder slides with themes, images, videos, tables, and charts. The app supports multi-presentation management, WYSIWYG editing, and presentation mode with scaling. Slides can be exported as PDF in landscape with formatting preserved.
PEN+
Pen+ is a digital whiteboard tool for teachers and students. It supports drawing tools, and annotation features. Teachers can create interactive lessons, share screens, and annotate presentations with ease.
